03 A letter to your congressperson

The Letter

An example of a filled out letter, and a template to use when writing your own.

Dear Congressman Peters,

We hope this letter finds you well. Congratulations on your recent partnership with Congressman Brian Fitzpatrick on promoting accessible affordable housing.

We’re writing to express our interests in promoting legislative and organizational reform for our Armed Forces to better address the growing innovations in maritime drug trafficking. We’ve crafted a bill and put together a website with additional information we think you may be interested in taking a look at.

While the Department of War has proven proactive in pivoting to address the diverse innovations made by our conventional adversaries, it is our belief that the narcotics trade has slipped from the calculus. While our warfighters have spent decades fighting diverse enemies across the globe, America’s war against drugs has remained the country’s most consistent, long-lasting conflict.

Congressman Peters, you’ve historically been a staunch advocate of supporting our warfighters with a robust military budget—you understand San Diego’s significance as a bastion of our Navy and Marine Corps. We hope that from this background, you will consider our bill.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration,

Ethan Leem and Kiran Narayanan

I am writing as a constituent to ask you to support and vote for the Halting and Reducing Proliferation of Ocean-based Narcotics (HARPOON) Act.

Maritime drug trafficking is becoming more sophisticated, with traffickers using technologies such as autonomous vessels, GPS-guided buoys, aerial drones, and satellite communications. These developments create gaps that existing approaches are not fully equipped to address.

The HARPOON Act would help coordinate existing military capabilities across air, land, sea, space, and cyber domains to strengthen counternarcotics operations. It would build on existing infrastructure and improve our ability to respond to evolving threats.

Please consider supporting the HARPOON Act and voting for its passage. Thank you for your time and for your service to our community.
